# Quality scenarios and measures

A base clause states an outcome. Its scenario prompts identify conditions worth exercising, and its evidence prompts identify observations that can establish the outcome. A concrete obligation remains complete only when its context and pass boundary are explicit.

## Scenario form

State the source of a stimulus, the stimulus, the affected artifact, the environment, the required observable response, and the response measure. Split scenarios when different contexts require different responses or pass boundaries.

## Measurement contract

A quantitative measure names the affected population, a good event, eligibility and exclusions, the objective, evaluation window, aggregation, measurement point, and the decision caused by a miss. Exact invariants use an exact pass condition. Continuous objectives may allow bounded misses only when the underlying obligation permits them.

## Evidence

Use the earliest evidence capable of establishing the clause: inspection, analysis, deterministic test, representative exercise, or observation at the affected boundary. Evidence supports a clause; it does not replace its stable identifier or affirmative obligation.
