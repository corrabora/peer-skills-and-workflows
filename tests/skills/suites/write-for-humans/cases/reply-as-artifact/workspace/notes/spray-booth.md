# Spray booth, points for the door notice

- The booth fan runs for the whole spraying session and for ten minutes
  after the last piece; the switch is on the wall to the left of the booth.
- Sprayed pieces go on the drying rack by the window, never on the kiln
  shelves, because wet glaze marks the shelves.
- The glaze monitor changes the booth filter on the first Tuesday of each
  month. If the filter is torn before then, tell the glaze monitor; do not
  change it yourself.
- Clean the booth floor with the wet sponge, not the brush, so that dry
  glaze does not go into the air.
