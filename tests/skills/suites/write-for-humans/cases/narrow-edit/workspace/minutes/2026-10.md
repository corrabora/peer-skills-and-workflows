# Committee meeting, 6 October 2026

Present: seven members, including the kiln steward.

## Collecting fired work

Decision: fired work must be collected from the cooling shelf within seven
days of the unloading note. After seven days the kiln steward moves it to
the overflow shelf in the store, where it is kept for one month; the member
is told by email when it is moved. The rule applies from 1 November 2026.

## Other business

- Clay orders for the winter term close on 20 October.
