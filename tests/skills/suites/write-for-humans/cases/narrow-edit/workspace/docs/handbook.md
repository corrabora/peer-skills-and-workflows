# Members' handbook

## Booking a firing

Book a slot in the kiln diary, which hangs by the kiln, and write each piece
in the kiln log with your initials. Pieces go on the booking shelf by 18:00
the day before the firing.

## Collecting fired work

Fired work is unloaded onto the cooling shelf by the kiln steward, who posts
an unloading note on the diary. Collect your pieces whenever the studio is
open. Work left on the cooling shelf for more than a month may be moved to
the store.

## Glaze room

Label every glaze bucket with its recipe code and the date it was mixed, and
wash the sieve after use. The glaze monitor keeps the house glazes.

## Keys and alarm

Members with a key set the alarm when they are the last to leave. The code
is on the card in the key safe.
