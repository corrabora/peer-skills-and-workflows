# Insurance summary, policy year 2026

A summary of the cover the cooperative holds, kept for members. The full
policy document is held by the treasurer.

- Buildings and contents: the studio, the kiln, and members' work on the
  premises up to a stated limit per member.
- Kiln records: a record of every firing must be kept and be available for
  inspection on request.
- Public liability: open days and workshops are covered when a member of the
  committee is present.
