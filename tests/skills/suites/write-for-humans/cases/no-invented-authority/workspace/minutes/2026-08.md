# Committee meeting, 4 August 2026

Present: eight members.

## Kiln log

Decision: from 1 January 2027 the paper kiln log is replaced by the shared
kiln-log spreadsheet. Members enter their pieces in the spreadsheet from the
studio laptop before putting them on the booking shelf. The paper log stays
in use until 31 December and is then archived in the store.

Who prints a monthly copy of the spreadsheet was not decided.
