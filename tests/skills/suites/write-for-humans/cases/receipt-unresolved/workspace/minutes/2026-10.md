# Committee meeting, 6 October 2026

Present: seven members, including the treasurer and the kiln steward.

## Fees from 1 January 2027

Decisions:

- Bisque firing: 6 pounds per full shelf, 3 pounds per half shelf.
- Glaze firing: 9 pounds per full shelf, 5 pounds per half shelf.
- Test tiles: no charge, up to six per member per firing, on the steward's
  test shelf.
- Private firing (one member booking the whole kiln): the treasurer will set
  the fee after the electricity contract is renewed in December. Until then
  no figure has been agreed.
- Fees are added to each member's quarterly invoice by the treasurer; nobody
  pays at the studio.

## Other business

- The glaze room sink is replaced in the week of 9 November; the glaze room
  is closed that week.
