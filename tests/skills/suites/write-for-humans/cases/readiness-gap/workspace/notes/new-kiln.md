# New kiln, installation notes

Installed on 3 September 2026 by the supplier's engineer. It is a 180-litre
front loader with a programmable controller, in the same position as the
old kiln, which the supplier took away. The first member firings ran in the
week of 7 September: two bisque, one glaze. All three reached temperature.

The steward has written the two standard programs (bisque to cone 06, glaze
to cone 6) into the controller as programs 1 and 2. Members do not change
the programs.
