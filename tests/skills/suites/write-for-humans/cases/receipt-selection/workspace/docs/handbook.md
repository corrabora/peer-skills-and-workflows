# Members' handbook

## Glaze room rules

1. Label every glaze bucket with the recipe code and the date it was mixed.
2. Wash the sieve and return it to the rack after use.
3. Run the spray booth fan for the whole spraying session.
4. Keep food and drink out of the glaze room.
5. Close the window when you leave.
6. Put sprayed pieces on the drying rack, not on the kiln shelves.
7. Wipe the bench down before you leave.
8. Tell the glaze monitor about any spill of dry glaze material.

## Booking a firing

Book a slot in the kiln diary and write each piece in the kiln log with your
initials.
