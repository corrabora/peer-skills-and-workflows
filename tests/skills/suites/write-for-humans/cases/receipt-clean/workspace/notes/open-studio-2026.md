# Open studio day 2026, planning notes

Date fixed: Saturday 21 November 2026, 10:00 to 16:00, the same hours as
last year.

Members bring their own bisque-fired pieces for the raku pit, anything up to
hand size; the pit takes about forty pieces over the day. Raku glazes are
provided from the glaze room; members do not bring their own.

Different this year:

- The car park is closed for resurfacing all weekend. Park on Mill Lane and
  walk in through the side gate, which is unlocked from 09:30.
- The raku pit moves from the yard to the field behind the studio because of
  the resurfacing.

Tea and coffee all day in the glaze room, as usual. The glaze monitor runs
the raku pit; the kiln steward gives the main kiln demonstration at 14:00,
as last year.
