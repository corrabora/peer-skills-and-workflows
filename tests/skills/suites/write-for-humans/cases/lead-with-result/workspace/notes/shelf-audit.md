# Shelf audit, September 2026 (kiln steward)

Background, for anyone new to this: the kiln is the front-loading electric
kiln bought in 2019, with eight shelf positions. Full shelves are 40 cm
square; half shelves are 40 by 20. The shelves live on the rack to the left
of the kiln, and the kiln wash is in the two tubs under the rack: the blue
tub for members, the grey tub for the steward's own mix.

How I did the audit: I took every shelf off the rack on 12 September, checked
each for cracks and glaze runs, re-ground two of them, then went through the
kiln diary for the last year to see which shelves get booked, and talked to
the glaze monitor about the test tiles. It took most of a Saturday.

Result:

- Full shelves A, B, D, E and F are for members' pieces.
- Full shelf C is withdrawn until it has been re-ground; it has a crack along
  one edge. Do not book it.
- Half shelves G and H are reserved: G for the steward's test tiles, H for
  the cooperative's cone packs. Members do not use them.
- Members coat shelves with kiln wash from the blue tub only. The grey tub
  is the steward's.

I will re-grind shelf C in October if the grinder is free.
