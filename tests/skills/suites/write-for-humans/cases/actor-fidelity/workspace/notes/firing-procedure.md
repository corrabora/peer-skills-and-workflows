# How a glaze firing runs, as agreed with the steward and the treasurer

Member: books a slot in the kiln diary (the diary hangs by the kiln; one
line per shelf). Pieces must be dry and bisque fired before booking.

Member: writes each piece in the kiln log with their initials and the glaze
codes used, and puts the pieces on the booking shelf by 18:00 the day
before the firing.

Glaze monitor: on the evening before the firing, checks every piece on the
booking shelf for glaze on the foot ring and sets aside any piece that has
some, with a note for the member. Those pieces are not fired.

Kiln steward: loads the kiln (members never load or unload it), sets the
program for the highest cone written in the log, and starts the firing.

Kiln steward: after the kiln has cooled, unloads it onto the cooling shelf
and posts the unloading note on the diary. Pieces are ready to collect when
the unloading note is up, usually two days after the firing.

Treasurer: adds the firing fee to the member's next quarterly invoice from
the kiln log.

Member: collects the pieces from the cooling shelf within a week of the
unloading note.
