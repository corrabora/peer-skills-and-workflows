# Quarterly meeting, 12 September 2026

Present: the coordinator, the records keeper, five gauge readers.

## Decisions

Accepted: from 1 October the archive records every river reading in
metres to one decimal place. The 2025 files were entered to two decimal
places and the data assistant is to bring them to one; a second decimal of
5 rounds up. Proposed in June, accepted at this meeting.

Proposed, not yet decided: that the archive drop the readers' initials
column from 2027. The records keeper will consult the readers and the
question returns to the December meeting. Nothing changes until then.

## Standing arrangements, restated for the record

The data assistant may create and change files under `archive/` and
`briefs/` on its own authority under its standing instructions. It asks
the records keeper only when a reading cannot be placed in a gauge record;
for anything else it proceeds.

## Other business

The W7 staff gauge is to be repainted before the winter; the coordinator
will arrange it with the reader.
