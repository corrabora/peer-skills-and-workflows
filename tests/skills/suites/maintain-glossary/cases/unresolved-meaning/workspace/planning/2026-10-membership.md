# Membership notes, October

The committee agreed in September that a household membership covers everyone
living at one address under one member number, with one tool on loan at a time
between them (`minutes/2026-09-committee.md`). The list has been updated.

"Active member" is used two ways. On the list, the membership secretary marks a
member active when the current year's fee has been paid. The loan rules open
bookings to active members and mean by it anyone who has borrowed in the last
twelve months (`docs/contracts/loan-rules.md`). Several members have paid this
year and not borrowed for over a year, and at least one borrowed last month on
a lapsed fee. The committee has not been asked which meaning should stand.
