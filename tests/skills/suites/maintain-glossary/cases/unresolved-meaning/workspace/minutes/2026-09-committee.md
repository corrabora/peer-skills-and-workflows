# Committee, September meeting

Present: the librarian, the membership secretary, two desk volunteers, one
bench volunteer.

1. Accounts. The accounts were received. No action.
2. Words on the desk sheet. The desk volunteers asked for the words on the
   desk sheet to mean one thing each. Agreed:
   - A booking is a member's request for a named tool on a future date, held
     at the desk until the tool is collected or the date passes.
   - A loan is a tool issued to a member at the desk, from issue until the
     tool is checked back in.
   - A loan is overdue from the day after its due date until the tool is
     checked in.
   The librarian to record these where volunteers will find them.
3. Catalogue spreadsheet. The status column now takes the values available,
   on loan, booked, at bench and missing. Rows are sorted by id. No change to
   the rules.
4. Shelving. The hand tools move to the lower shelves before the autumn
   stocktake.
5. Household membership. Agreed: a household membership covers everyone
   living at one address under one member number, with one tool on loan at a
   time between them. The membership secretary to update the list.
