# Overdue proposal

From the desk volunteers, for the committee's next meeting.

Members returning a tool a day or two late say they did not know the due date
had passed, and the desk spends time arguing over a 1.00 fee. We propose that a
loan counts as overdue only from the fourth day after its due date, and that
the first three days carry no fee. The librarian would need to change the
written words volunteers are given once the committee has decided. Not yet put
to the committee.
