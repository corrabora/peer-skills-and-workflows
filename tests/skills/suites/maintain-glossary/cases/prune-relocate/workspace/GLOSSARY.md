# Glossary

## Booking

A member's request for a named tool on a future date, held at the desk until the tool is collected or the date passes.

## Loan

A tool issued to a member at the desk, from issue until it is checked back in.

## Overdue

A loan that has passed its due date without the tool being checked in. The fee is 1.00 per day for a hand tool and 2.50 per day for a power tool, collected at return. The fee for one loan is capped at 25.00. The fee is waived when the tool was recalled for repair before its due date.

## Renewal

A loan extended once at the desk for a further seven days when nobody has booked the tool.
