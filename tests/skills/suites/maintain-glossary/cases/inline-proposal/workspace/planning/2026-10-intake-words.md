# Intake words

For the committee. The bench and the desk agree on these two:

- An intake slip is the form a donor fills in when handing a tool to the desk,
  recording the donor, the tool and any known fault.
- A bench check is the bench's assessment of a deposited tool before it enters
  the catalogue, ending in accept, repair first or reject.

They do not agree on "scrap". The intake rules say a rejected tool is scrapped
with the donor's agreement (`docs/contracts/intake.md`), meaning disposed of.
The bench says a tool it scraps is kept for parts and never leaves the
building. A rejected drill kept for its motor is scrapped under one reading and
not under the other. The committee has not decided.
