# Loan rules

A loan begins when a desk volunteer issues a tool to a member and ends when the
tool is checked back in at the desk. The standard loan period is seven days; a
loan can be renewed once at the desk for a further seven days if nobody has
booked the tool.

A tool is overdue from the day after its due date. The overdue fee is 1.00 per
day for a hand tool and 2.50 per day for a power tool, collected at return.

Power tools are issued against a hire deposit of 20.00, held at the desk and
returned with the tool when it comes back complete and undamaged.
