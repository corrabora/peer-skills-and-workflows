# Bench words

Agreed at the bench by all five bench volunteers, for the bench's own notes:

- A bench ticket is the card tied to a tool while it is at the bench, carrying
  the fault reported and the work done.
- A service hold is a tool kept off the shelf by the bench while it waits for
  parts or a safety check; the desk may not issue it.
- A strip-down is the complete disassembly of a power tool to find a fault,
  done only when a test by hand has not found it.
- A write-off is a tool the bench has judged beyond economic repair; it leaves
  the catalogue once the librarian is told.
- A parts bin is the labelled box of serviceable parts recovered from
  write-offs, kept for repairs only and never issued.

Separately, the desk volunteers use "hold" for a booked tool set aside at the
desk until the member collects it, and have asked for a list of their own with
hold in it so that the desk and the bench stop talking past each other. The
bench has no view on where that word goes.
