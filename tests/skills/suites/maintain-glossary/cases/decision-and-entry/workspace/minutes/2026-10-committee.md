# Committee, October meeting

Present: the librarian, the membership secretary, two desk volunteers.

1. Membership. The membership secretary reported that several addresses hold
   two or three memberships and borrow in turn to get round the one tool at a
   time rule. Agreed: a membership covers one household, everyone living at
   one address under one member number, with one tool on loan at a time
   between them; the yearly fee is unchanged. In the glossary and library
   records, “member” now names this household; “membership” names its
   subscription. The membership secretary to
   merge the duplicate memberships; the librarian to record the decision and
   bring the written words into line.
2. Shelving. Done.
