# Terms

Words used on the desk sheet and in the rules, kept by the librarian.

### booking

- Means: A member's request for a named tool on a future date, held at the desk until the tool is collected or the date passes.
- Used in: desk sheet, loan rules.
- Status: agreed by the committee.

### grace period

- Means: The first three days after a due date, proposed to carry no fee.
- Used in: desk proposal only.
- Status: proposed by the desk volunteers, not yet before the committee.

### loan

- Means: A tool issued to a member at the desk, from issue until it is checked back in.
- Used in: loan rules.
- Status: agreed by the committee.

### overdue

- Means: A loan that has passed its due date without the tool being checked in.
- Used in: loan rules.
- Status: agreed by the committee.

### write-off

- Means: A tool the bench has judged beyond economic repair and removed from the catalogue once the librarian is told.
- Used in: bench notes.
- Status: agreed by the committee.
