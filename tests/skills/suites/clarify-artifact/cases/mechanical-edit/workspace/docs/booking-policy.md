# Booking policy

Members and guests book through the hut secretary. Bookings are confirmed by
email once payment is recieved; the confirmation carries the key-safe code.
Accomodation is in shared bunk rooms, and the association does not reserve
whole huts for private parties between May and September.

## Cancelling a booking

1. Email the hut secretary with the booking reference.
2. If the cancellation reaches the secretary at least fourteen days before the
   booked night, the full payment is refunded.
2. If it reaches the secretary between three and fourteen days before, half is
   refunded.
4. Within three days of the booked night nothing is refunded, unless the Fell
   Road is closed by the highway authority on the booked date.
6. The secretary confirms the refund by email and updates the calender.
