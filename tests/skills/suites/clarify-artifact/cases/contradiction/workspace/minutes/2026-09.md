# Committee meeting, 9 September 2026

Present: chair, treasurer, hut secretary, three wardens.

1. Minutes of the August meeting were accepted.
2. Sour Beck footbridge: the replacement is complete; final invoice £2,150.
3. Dogs. Agreed: dogs are welcome at Kestrel hut, kept on a lead in the porch

   path crosses the ground-nesting bird area from April to July and because
