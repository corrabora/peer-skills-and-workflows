# Kestrel hut water supply (working notes)

The hut's water comes from a spring through a 900-litre tank behind the hut.

## August test

The laboratory sample taken on 12 August showed coliform bacteria above the
guideline. The committee agreed on 20 August that all drinking water is to be
boiled until a tank treatment has been done and a retest has passed.

## Tank treatment (supplier's procedure, carried out by the maintenance volunteer)

1. Drain the tank to the overflow mark and scrub the walls.
2. Refill, add 90 ml of the supplied chlorine solution, and leave for twelve
   hours with every tap closed.
3. Flush every tap until the chlorine smell has gone.
4. Take a retest sample in the supplied bottle and post it the same day.

## Requests

Overnight guests have asked for something written that tells them whether the
water is safe and what to do. The maintenance volunteer has asked for the
treatment procedure written up so that a stand-in can follow it. Nothing has
been written for either yet.
