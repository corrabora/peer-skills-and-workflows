# Kestrel hut stove change (working notes)

Status: proposed. The committee votes at the October meeting.

## The proposal

Replace the wood stove with a bottled-gas heater. Reasons: the flue failed its
August inspection and a like-for-like repair is quoted at £1,400; wood delivery
by quad bike costs £620 a season and depends on one volunteer; the supplier's
heater and fitting are quoted at £1,900 with bottle exchange at £38.

Objections raised so far: bottles would come up the same track as the wood
did; nobody has worked out where full and empty bottles would be stored.

## Operating steps drafted by the supplier (for wardens, if the change goes ahead)

1. Check the bottle connection with soapy water before the first lighting of a
   turn.
2. Open the bottle valve a quarter turn, then press and hold the igniter for
   ten seconds.
3. Shut the bottle valve at the end of each turn, not just the heater control.
4. If gas is smelt: shut the bottle valve, open both windows, and do not use
   the igniter or a torch until the smell has gone.
