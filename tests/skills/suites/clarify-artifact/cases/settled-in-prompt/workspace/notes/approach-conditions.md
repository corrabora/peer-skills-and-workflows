# Approach conditions: Fell Road car park to Kestrel hut (warden notes, October)

- Distance 6 km, ascent 520 m. Three hours is typical in winter for a fit
  walker; allow four in fresh snow.
- The footbridge over Sour Beck is lifted out from the first week of November
  to the end of March so that ice does not carry it away. In winter the beck
  is forded at the marked stones 40 m upstream. After heavy rain or a thaw the
  ford is impassable, sometimes for two days.
- No phone signal beyond the old quarry, about 2 km in.
- The path above the quarry crosses two short slopes that hold hard snow from
  December. Microspikes have been enough every year so far; nobody has needed
  crampons on this approach.
- Daylight in January ends about 16:20. A head torch with spare batteries is
  needed for anyone leaving the car park after 12:30.
- The key-safe code is sent with the booking confirmation; there is no key at
  the car park.
- Wood for the stove is already in the hut; guests carry their own food,
  water for the walk and a sleeping bag.
