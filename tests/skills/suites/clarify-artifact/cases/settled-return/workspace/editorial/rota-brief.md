# Brief: warden rota notice, winter 2026

- Reader: the twelve volunteer wardens.
- Purpose: tell each warden which fortnight is theirs between November and
  March, and what to do if they cannot cover it, so that no turn goes
  uncovered.
- Scope: the winter rota only; summer turns are agreed at the April meeting.
- Constraints: one side of A4, pinned in both huts and sent by email; names
  and dates are taken from `rota/winter-2026.csv` as agreed on 9 September;
  swaps go through the hut secretary, never directly between wardens.
