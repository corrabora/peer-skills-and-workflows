# Route survey, September 2026

Fell Road route: open. The Sour Beck footbridge was replaced in May; the new
bridge is wider and has a handrail. Waymarks repainted in July.

Quarry Track: open. The route was re-waymarked in August after the forestry
clearance moved the path 200 m west of the old line between the quarry gate and
the sheepfold. Old cairns on the felled ground have been removed.

North Gully: closed until further notice. The scree slope below the gully
mouth moved in the June storm and the survey party could not establish a safe
line. A re-survey is planned for spring 2027.
