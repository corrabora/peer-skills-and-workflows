# Plots committee, 7 October 2026

1. Rent by area. Half plots and the two double plots have been charged by
   plot for thirty years, and three disputes this year turned on what counts
   as a half. Decided: from the 2027 rent year, rent is charged per square
   metre from the measured area in the register, every plot is measured by
   the end of January, and the three rent bands are withdrawn. The treasurer
   noted that twelve holders' rent will rise and nine will fall; the
   committee accepted that and will write to each. Reversing it later would
   mean re-issuing every rent letter and reinstating the bands.
2. The noticeboard will be repainted green rather than brown.
3. The monthly meeting moves from the first Tuesday to the first Wednesday
   from January.
4. The register keeper will add a check to the register script that rejects
   a duplicate plot number.
5. Water. The standpipes are turned off on 1 November under the water
   supplier's terms, as every year.
6. Renewals: 14 of 52 plots have renewed so far; letters to the rest go out
   next week.
