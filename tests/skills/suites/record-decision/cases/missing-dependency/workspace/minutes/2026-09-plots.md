# Plots committee, 9 September 2026

## Lane boundary

The hawthorn hedge on the lane side harbours rats that reach the plots
nearest the lane, and cutting it takes two volunteer days a year. Decided,
six votes to one: replace the hedge with a post-and-wire fence over the
winter, accepting the loss of shelter on the lane-side plots. The
contractor's quote is 2,100.

## Bank mandate

The treasurer's proposal to change the bank mandate goes to the finance
meeting on 23 September; it is not a matter for this committee.

## Other business

The noticeboard will be repainted green. The November meeting moves to the
12th because the hall is booked on the 5th.
