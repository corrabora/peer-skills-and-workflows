# Plots committee, 9 September 2026

Present: five committee members, the register keeper, the water steward.

## Toilet

The chemical toilet's emptying contract ends in December. Three options were
discussed. A mains connection: the quote is 11,400 and the society's reserve
is 6,200, so it would need a loan the committee will not take on. Keeping the
chemical toilet: emptying has risen to 1,900 a year and the contractor will
not commit beyond one year. A composting toilet: 3,800 to buy and install, no
emptying contract, but a volunteer rota to rake and empty the chamber every
fortnight.

The water steward said a rota will be hard to fill in winter. The chair said
the rota is the price of not paying 1,900 a year. Two members preferred the
mains quote with a loan.

Decided, five votes to two: replace the chemical toilet with a composting
toilet when the emptying contract ends, accepting a fortnightly volunteer
rota for the chamber. The water steward will draw up the rota before
December.

## Other business

The noticeboard will be repainted green. The November meeting moves to the
12th because the hall is booked on the 5th.
