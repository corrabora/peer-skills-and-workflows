# Plots committee, 7 October 2026

## Beehives

An applicant asked to keep two hives on plot 22. The society's rules say
nothing about bees. The committee proposes to allow hives on the two plots
at the far end, 22 and 23, and nowhere else, with the keeper's insurance
shown each year, because stings near the children's plots by the gate were
the objection raised. A rule change needs a members' vote at the annual
general meeting on 21 November. No hives before then.
