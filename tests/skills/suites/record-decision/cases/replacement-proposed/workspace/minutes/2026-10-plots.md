# Plots committee, 7 October 2026

## Waiting-list order

Two committee members proposed ordering the waiting list by distance from
the site, nearest first, instead of by application date, because applicants
from outside the parish have taken four of the last six plots while parish
residents wait, and the lease with the parish council expects the society to
serve the parish. The chair noted that the current order has been in force
since 2024 and that changing it moves people already on the list. A change
to waiting-list order needs a vote of the members, so the proposal goes to
the annual general meeting on 21 November. The list stays in
application-date order until then.
