# Decisions log

Kept by the register keeper since 2012. From 2025 new decisions are filed in
`docs/adr/`; D-8 and D-9 were entered here in 2026 by the previous keeper
before the change was passed on.

## D-1 (2012): Plot rent collected annually

Status: accepted
Context: Quarterly collection produced four reminder rounds a year.
Decision: Rent is collected once a year, due on 1 April.
Consequences: One letter round; holders who leave mid-year are not refunded.

## D-2 (2014): Half plots let to new applicants first

Status: accepted
Context: Full plots were given up unworked within a year by a third of new holders.
Decision: A new applicant is offered a half plot first.
Consequences: Full plots go to holders who have worked a half plot for a year.

## D-3 (2016): No bonfires between April and October

Status: accepted
Context: Complaints from the houses on the lane during summer.
Decision: Bonfires are allowed only from November to March.
Consequences: Green waste is composted or taken away in summer.

## D-4 (2019): Standpipes locked over winter

Status: accepted
Context: Two burst pipes in the 2018 frost.
Decision: The water steward turns off and drains the standpipes on 1 November.
Consequences: No water on site from November to March.

## D-5 (2023): Shed key held by the water steward

Status: accepted
Context: Three keys were lost in two years.
Decision: One key, held by the water steward; the shed is opened on work days.
Consequences: Tools are not available outside work days.

## D-8 (2026): Hedge cutting in February only

Status: accepted
Context: Nesting birds in the lane hedge.
Decision: The lane hedge is cut in February and at no other time.
Consequences: A heavy growth year goes uncut until the next February.

## D-9 (2026): Skip hired once a year

Status: accepted
Context: Fly-tipped waste built up by the gate.
Decision: A skip is hired for one weekend each October.
Consequences: Waste is kept on plots until the skip weekend.
