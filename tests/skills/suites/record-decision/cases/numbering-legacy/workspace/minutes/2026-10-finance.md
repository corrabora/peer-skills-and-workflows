# Finance meeting, 14 October 2026

## Two signatories

After the duplicated payment to the fencing contractor in August, decided:
every payment above 250 needs two signatories from the committee, the
treasurer and one other, because a single signatory cannot catch a repeated
invoice; a delay of up to a week on larger payments is accepted. The
treasurer will change the bank mandate.

## Other business

The accounts for 2025 were approved for the annual general meeting.
