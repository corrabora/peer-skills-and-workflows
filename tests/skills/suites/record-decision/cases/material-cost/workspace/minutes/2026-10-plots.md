# Plots committee, 7 October 2026

## Plot boundaries

The 1998 survey pegs still mark every plot. A resurvey was quoted at 4,800
and 5,300 by two surveyors; the society's reserve is 6,200 and the committee
will not spend most of it on this. The register keeper reminded the meeting
that the 1998 drawing is known to be half a metre out on the east row, so
plots 14, 15 and 16 are each about half a metre narrower on the ground than
the register says; the holder of plot 15 has raised this twice.

Decided, five votes to two: plot boundaries follow the 1998 survey pegs and
the register's areas are the 1998 figures, and the east-row error stands
until a resurvey the society can afford. The holders of plots 14 to 16 will
be told in writing.

## Other business

The noticeboard will be repainted green. The November meeting moves to the
12th because the hall is booked on the 5th.
