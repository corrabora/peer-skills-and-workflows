# Plots committee, 7 October 2026

## Double plots

Plots 3 and 11 are double plots held since the 1990s; both holders have said
they will give up half when asked. Forty-one applicants are waiting and most
ask for a half plot. The committee proposes to split both double plots into
four half plots when the current holders give notice, with the halves let in
waiting-list order, because two double plots serve two households while
forty-one wait. Under the society's rules a change to plot sizes needs a vote
of the members, so the proposal goes to the annual general meeting on 21
November. Nothing changes before then.

The treasurer asked whether the rent bands cover half plots; they do.

## Other business

The noticeboard will be repainted green. The November meeting moves to the
12th because the hall is booked on the 5th.
